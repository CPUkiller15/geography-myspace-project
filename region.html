Region

Areas grouped together because they share something in common.

What Region Means

Regions are places grouped together by similarities — weather, culture, vibes, anything.

Formal Region

Official boundaries like states or countries.

Functional Region

Built around a central point — like a school district.

Vernacular Region

Based on how people *think* about a place — like “the South.”
