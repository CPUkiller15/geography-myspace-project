Human–Environment Interaction

How humans and the environment affect each other.

What HEI Means

We depend on nature, adapt to it, and change it. Sometimes we help it, sometimes we totally ruin it (oops).

Depend

We need water, air, land, and resources.

Adapt

We change our clothes, houses, and habits based on climate.

Change

We build roads, cities, farms, and more.
